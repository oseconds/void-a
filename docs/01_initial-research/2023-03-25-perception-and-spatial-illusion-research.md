# 2023.03.25 Perception and Spatial Illusion Research

## Research Direction

The project explored how the concept of gaze could be translated into a spatial experience.

The question shifted from:
"How can a viewer see a virtual object?"

to:

"How can a virtual object appear to exist differently depending on the viewer's position?"

## Conceptual Connection

A fixed image cannot create the experience of gaze.

The artwork needs to change according to the observer's position so that perception itself becomes part of the work.
